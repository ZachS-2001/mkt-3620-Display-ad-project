Campaign at a glance

The strategy behind the creative.

GOAL

Drive rental requests

Every ad and the landing page push one action: submit a rental request.

PRIMARY AUDIENCE

Iowa State visitors and families

Parents, alumni, and fans visiting Ames. Secondary: locals who need a car for a few days, and students who can rent in their own name.

THE HOOK

“Need wheels in Ames?”

A short local question, varied slightly by ad size (for example, “Rent local in Ames.”).

CORE MESSAGE

Affordable, local, easy to request

Affordable car rentals in Ames, Iowa. Rides to DSM Airport. First come, first served.
